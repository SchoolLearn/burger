What to know about ReaperX, where we're headed, and what's available today.

Can I download ReaperX today?

Not yet. ReaperX is in development, and a public installer is not currently available through this website. We'll publish confirmed system requirements, release details, and a download when the product is ready.

What is ReaperX being built to do?

The roadmap includes Windows malware scanning and removal, real-time protection, AI-assisted threat detection, quarantine, and device monitoring. These are development goals; the capabilities in the first public release will be confirmed separately.

How will the free trial and subscription work?

The proposed offer is seven days free, followed by a $6 USD monthly subscription. Enrollment is not open. Payment timing, renewal, cancellation, refund terms, and any applicable charges will be explained before you sign up.

Does this website scan or protect my device?

No. This is the ReaperX product website. The interface shown above is a design concept, and browsing this page does not install an antivirus, run a scan, or assess your device's security.

Which devices will ReaperX support?

ReaperX is being designed for Windows PCs. Supported Windows versions, hardware requirements, and device allowances will be specified with the public release.

Will ReaperX offer VPN or business protection?

VPN services and business cybersecurity are future areas of interest. They are not currently available and are not represented as included in the proposed antivirus plan.
